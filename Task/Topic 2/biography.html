APJ Abdul Kalam

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India" because of his important contribution to India's space and missile programs. He strongly believed that dreams can become reality through hard work, determination, and action. His inspiring words encouraged millions of students to dream big, work hard, and never give up on their goals. He believed that young people are the future of the nation and always motivated them to develop knowledge, confidence, and a strong vision for India.
